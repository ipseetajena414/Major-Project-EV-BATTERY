# RAG literature sources

**No papers are bundled and none are claimed to be indexed.** The corpus starts empty; the page says so until you upload PDFs.

- Use open-access papers (e.g. from arXiv) or files you are legally allowed to use. Do not upload paywalled full text you cannot use.
- Title is required. Authors, year, DOI/arXiv id and URL are whatever you type; blank fields stay blank and are shown as "not provided".
- Retrieval is local TF-IDF (lexical), not neural semantic search; paraphrased questions may miss relevant passages.
- Scanned PDFs without a text layer cannot be indexed (no OCR).
- Answers show verbatim excerpts with source, page and score. The optional Gemini interpretation (needs `GOOGLE_API_KEY`) is shown separately and was not exercised in testing.
- Priority topics to collect: solid-state (sulfide/oxide electrolytes, interfaces, dendrites, critical current density, impedance, stack pressure, degradation) and sodium-ion (layered oxide, polyanionic, Prussian blue cathodes, cycle life, low-temperature behaviour, energy density).
